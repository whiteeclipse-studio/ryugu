The user has selected December 15 as the Tokyo departure date. The [current Tokyo budget](chapter-01-finding-my-place.md#chapter-recap) reflects that choice. The comparison below is retained only as historical context for the unselected December 8 option; it is not part of this chapter's current budget or an open date decision. At the Tokyo sharehouse ceiling of ¥100,000 per 30-day month, seven fewer nights represent approximately ¥23,333 in assumed rent savings, subject to actual billing and notice terms.

# CHAPTER 01 — finding my pl\[A\]ce

*Settling In*

## Summary

This first chapter runs from **October 19 to December 15, 2027**, beginning with the journey from Strasbourg to Tokyo and arrival in Japan on **October 20**. Tokyo will serve as the base for settling into Japanese life, completing arrival formalities, establishing everyday routines, and adapting working arrangements while beginning to explore the city.

By the end of the chapter, the aim is to feel comfortable living and working in Tokyo before beginning the journey north through Ibaraki, Tohoku, and Hokkaido.

Duration: **57 calendar days**, including the start date and excluding the end date; **56 days in Japan** from October 20.

Part of the [travel plan](../travel-plan/README.md).

## Expected stay

### Hotel Paul House

The first three nights in Tokyo are planned at [Hotel Paul House near Ueno](https://www.booking.com/hotel/jp/tsubaki-tokyo.html), from **October 20 to October 23, 2027**. This stay provides a comfortable base for resting after the journey and settling in.

### Oakhouse sharehouse

From **October 23 to December 15, 2027 (53 nights)**, the plan is to stay in a private room in an [Oakhouse sharehouse](https://www.oakhouse.jp/eng) in Tokyo. A desk in the room is not required.

## Transportation

### Arrival

The inbound journey is planned with Emirates in Premium Economy on both flights. **EK044** departs Frankfurt Airport (FRA) at **11:00 on October 19, 2027**, arriving in Dubai (DXB) at **19:15 the same day**.

After a **7-hour 25-minute layover** and a change of aircraft in Dubai, **EK318** departs at **02:40 on October 20, 2027**, arriving at Tokyo Narita Airport (NRT) at **17:35 the same day**.

The total journey time from Frankfurt to Narita is **23 hours 35 minutes**. All times are local to the relevant airport.

The Premium Economy baggage allowance is **35 kg of checked baggage in total**, with a maximum of **32 kg per bag**, plus **10 kg of carry-on baggage**.

# CHAPTER 01 — finding my pl\[A\]ce

## Summary

This first chapter runs from **October 19 to December 15, 2027**, beginning with the journey from Strasbourg to Tokyo and arrival in Japan on **October 20**. Tokyo will serve as the base for settling into Japanese life, completing arrival formalities, establishing everyday routines, and adapting working arrangements while beginning to explore the city.

By the end of the chapter, the aim is to feel comfortable living and working in Tokyo before moving on to the next chapter, whose itinerary is TBD.

Duration: **57 calendar days**, including the start date and excluding the end date; **56 days in Japan** from October 20.

## Activities

### Tokyo

#### Sightseeing

The stay will provide time to tour Tokyo and experience everyday life across its neighbourhoods, particularly **Shinjuku, Shibuya, Ikebukuro, Minato, and Asakusa/Sumida**. Walks, meals, and repeated visits will allow time to get to know each area and its atmosphere.

**Places to revisit** include:

- The **Imperial Palace**, **Ueno**, and **Asakusa**, including **Senso-ji**, alongside another visit to **Tokyo Skytree**.
- **Meiji Jingu**, **Shibuya 109**, **Kabukicho**, and **Ikebukuro**.
- **Tokyo Tower**, plus **Miraikan** in Odaiba.
- **Inokashira Park**, with time to wander around Kichijoji as well.

**First-time visits** will include **Dogenzaka**, as part of a self-guided *The World Ends With You* location tour, and more of **Minato** beyond the places already visited.

**Todoroki**, including the valley and Todoroki Fudoson, and an **aquarium visit** are also on the list of places to discover.

Specific optional outings are listed in [Preparation and open decisions](#preparation-and-open-decisions).

#### Work

Two weeks off are planned around departure, covering the preceding week and the departure week. Work is planned to resume on October 25.

The expected workload is **30 hours during the last week of October**, **60 hours in November**, and **60 hours in December before December 15**, for a total of **150 hours** during this chapter.

This first stay in Tokyo will provide time to adapt to working with a **seven- to eight-hour time difference** and find a sustainable balance between professional commitments and exploring the city. Particular attention will go to coordinating meetings and availability across time zones, managing potentially late working hours, and preserving time for rest and everyday life.

The intended working routine uses **cafés and coworking spaces around Tokyo**, ideally varying venues to experience different neighbourhoods. No work venue is confirmed.

Venue selection remains TBD; see [Preparation and open decisions](#preparation-and-open-decisions) for the research shortlist.

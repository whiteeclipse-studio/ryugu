# Settling In
## Summary

This first chapter runs from **October 19 to December 15, 2027**, beginning with the journey from Strasbourg to Tokyo and arrival in Japan on **October 20**. Tokyo will serve as the base for settling into Japanese life, completing arrival formalities, establishing everyday routines, and adapting working arrangements while beginning to explore the city.

By the end of the chapter, the aim is to feel comfortable living and working in Tokyo before beginning the journey north through Ibaraki, Tohoku, and Hokkaido.

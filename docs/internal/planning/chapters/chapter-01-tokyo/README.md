# Chapter 01 — Tokyo — finding my feet
## Summary

This first stay in Tokyo provides time to settle into Japanese life, complete the required administrative tasks, and get used to everyday life in the city. The accommodation plan starts with a hotel from Wednesday to Saturday for comfort, followed by a private room in a sharehouse.

Two weeks off are planned, covering the week before departure and the departure week, with a likely Tuesday or Wednesday flight in the second week. The following two weeks of work will focus on adapting workflows to the anticipated seven- to eight-hour time difference. Working from cafés around Tokyo will leave room for exploring the city and trying restaurants at lunchtime.

Exploration will initially focus on Tokyo and its surroundings, potentially including a couple of days in Yokohama or Chiba. Kamakura and other similar day trips are reserved for the [second Tokyo chapter](../chapter-09-tokyo-2/README.md).

> [!source] Planning basis
> Dates, personal preferences, and budget targets come from the chapter discussion on October 19, 2026. They describe intended plans, not confirmed bookings or supplier quotes. The anticipated seven- to eight-hour time difference is a planning estimate pending date-specific verification.

### Flight to Tokyo

The inbound Emirates flight in **Premium Economy** has a one-time ticket budget of **€1,200 (¥192,000)**, separate from the recurring monthly allowances.

### Daily transport in Tokyo

Use the higher-end allowance of **¥1,200 per day** for **two return journeys** (four single journeys), at ¥300 per single journey. For reference, [Tokyo Metro’s regular adult fares](https://www.tokyometro.jp/lang_en/ticket/types/regular/index.html) range from ¥180 to ¥330 depending on distance, checked on October 19, 2026.

Over a 30-day month, this represents **¥36,000 (€230)**, counted within the monthly transport allowance rather than added to it.
